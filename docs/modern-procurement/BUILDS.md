# Modern Procurement — seven-build execution tracker

The approved sequence is fixed. Fixes and reruns belong to their originating build. No work starts on a later build until the preceding one passes its capability gate; no Build 8 is authorized.

| 6 | TCO and Should-Cost | Queued behind Build 5. |
| 7 | AP Invoice Ingestion and Programme Closeout | Queued behind Build 6. Includes integrated verification, evidence and the hard stop. |

Follow the programme definition of done and the evidence in STATUS and NEXT. Keep implementation, enablement and release sign-off distinct. A failed test is not a pass; a blocked build remains the current build. No business migrations, external delivery, real payments, or automatic commits are authorized by this tracker.
